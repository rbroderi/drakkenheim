#### Old Rattlecan

Sitting behind the Bark and Buzzard like a scarecrow, Old Rattlecan is an intelligent suit of [animated armour](https://www.dndbeyond.com/monsters/16786-animated-armor) which was found in the ruins of Drakkenheim. A mysterious magical enchantment causes it to rebuild itself when destroyed:

- Invites Harran to “come kick the can out back”. They challenge them to see which character can defeat Old Rattlecan the fastest, or who can survive the longest before being knocked out. Adding handicaps or bizarre “house rules” are popular ways to spice up the contest, such as being blindfolded, using no weapons, or taking drinks between hits.
- Rattlecan rejuvenates after one minute when reduced to 0 hit points unless it is [disintegrated](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2070-disintegrate) or [dispel magic](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2072-dispel-magic) is cast on its remains. Everyone in town is terribly upset if this happens.
- A creature reduced to 0 hit points by Old Rattlecan is [unconscious](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/free-rules/rules-glossary#UnconsciousCondition) but stable and wakes up one minute later with 1 hit point.
- Old Rattlecan can occasionally be heard making remarks to those it fights. It yells things like “Have at thee cur!”, “‘Tis a flesh wound!” or “Was that the best you can do?” as it seems to revel in battling any challenger.

### The path under the city.
If they take the smuggler's tunnel under the city it heads towards Slaughter Stone square. It runs for sometime as a separate tunnel but sometime it leads into part of the sewers once it gets close to Slaughter Stone square. They come across several Ratlings with dirty "cloaks" made from some sort of cloth or bedsheet. They are emblazoned with a symbol [[symbol_of_mouse.jpg]]. If the party introduces themselves with the agreed upon symbol they will not attack and invite the part to join them. They are sitting around a fire with "tuna" smoking at it. It smells really good and the party is invited to join them to eat.

Later down the tunnel the party runs into a group of fish people who are looking for one of their group, they can be reasoned with if the party recognizes the authority of the dutchess.
